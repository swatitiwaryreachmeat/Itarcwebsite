Country Overview

Why France for Indian students?

France combines world-class education, affordable public university fees, and a 2-year post-study work permit — making it ideal for fashion, business, engineering and arts students.

✓
Extremely low public university fees — as low as €170/yr (~₹16,200) for public universities, the most affordable in Western Europe.
✓
2-year post-study permit (APS) — stay in France for 2 years after graduation to find work, with no sponsorship needed.
✓
Grandes Écoles — world's elite — HEC Paris, Sciences Po, École Polytechnique rank among the world's top institutions for business and engineering.
✓
300+ English-taught Masters — no French required for most business and tech programs at top schools.
✓
Fashion & luxury capital — the only country to launch a career in luxury fashion, LVMH, L'Oréal, Chanel, Hermès headquarters.
✓
Campus France India pathway — dedicated government program with pre-visa academic support specifically for Indian students.

Visa Guidance

France student visa process

✓
Campus France registration (mandatory) — Indian students must register on CampusFrance India portal first. ITARC guides you through this.
✓
Long-stay student visa (VLS-TS) — apply at the French consulate in India after receiving your admission letter.
✓
Financial proof required — show €615/month (~₹58,775) of available funds for the duration of studies.
✓
APS post-study permit — after graduation, apply for 2-year Autorisation Provisoire de Séjour to seek employment without a job offer.
✓
Processing time: 3–5 weeks — apply at least 6–8 weeks before your course start date.

Student Lifestyle

Life as a student in France

🗼
Cultural Immersion
Paris is the fashion, art and gastronomic capital of the world — an extraordinary backdrop to student life. Every weekend is a new museum, market or festival.
🥐
Quality of Life
French café culture, 35-hour work weeks and world-famous cuisine make France one of the most liveable countries in Europe.
🚄
Central Location
TGV high-speed trains connect Paris to London (2.5hrs), Brussels (1.5hrs), Amsterdam (3hrs) and Barcelona (6hrs).
🏥
Free Healthcare
All students in France are entitled to the French social security system — near-free healthcare once enrolled at a university.

Country Overview

Culture, student life & popular cities

🎭 Culture & Student Life

France blends intellectual rigour with an unmatched quality of life. Students enjoy long lunch breaks, vibrant cafe culture, world-class museums (free for under-26), and annual student festivals. Classes are rigorous but social life is rich.

Work Opportunities

Students can work 964 hrs/year. LVMH, Hermes, Airbus and major multinationals actively recruit international graduates. Station F in Paris is the world largest startup campus.
