Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Flygrad — A Division of ITARC Business, Mumbai

1. Who We Are

Flygrad (a division of ITARC Business) is a study abroad consultancy based at 203 Patil Apartments, Gopi Tank Road, Mahim, Mumbai — 400016, India. We can be reached at info@itarcbusiness.com or +91 62915 27895.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use our website or services, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number (from contact forms)
  • Documents — passports, bank statements, academic transcripts (submitted via our upload portal for visa processing purposes only)
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type (via Google Analytics)
  • Communication data — messages sent via WhatsApp or email

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide study abroad counselling and visa guidance services
  • To process your university and visa applications
  • To send you relevant updates about your application status
  • To improve our website and services (analytics only)
  • To respond to your enquiries via email, phone or WhatsApp

4. Document Storage & Security

All documents you upload are stored securely in Google Drive with restricted access. Only authorised Flygrad counsellors can access your documents. We do not share your documents with any third party without your explicit written consent, except universities and embassies as required for your application.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for a maximum of 3 years after your last interaction with us, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@itarcbusiness.com.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

7. Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. These are anonymous analytics cookies only. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
