What is a Blocked Account?

A Sperrkonto is a German bank account where you deposit the full required amount before your visa interview. You can only withdraw €934/month after arriving in Germany — proving to the embassy you can fund your stay.

Visa Guidance

France student visa process

✓
Campus France registration (mandatory) — Indian students must register on CampusFrance India portal first. ITARC guides you through this.
✓
Long-stay student visa (VLS-TS) — apply at the French consulate in India after receiving your admission letter.
✓
Financial proof required — show €615/month (~₹58,775) of available funds for the duration of studies.
✓
APS post-study permit — after graduation, apply for 2-year Autorisation Provisoire de Séjour to seek employment without a job offer.
✓
Processing time: 3–5 weeks — apply at least 6–8 weeks before your course start date.

Study in Germany for Free — Complete India Handbook 2025

The world's best-kept study secret: tuition-free education, block account guide, APS certificate, and 18-month job seeker visa — all explained for Indian students.

Why Germany is the ultimate budget study destination

Germany abolished tuition fees at all public universities in 2014. Today, Indian students can attend TU Munich — a QS top-50 university — for just €150 per semester (about ₹14,000). The total study cost for a 2-year Masters in Germany is often less than a single semester at a UK or US university.

⚠️ APS Certificate — Mandatory for India

Indian students MUST obtain an APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) certificate before applying to German universities. The process takes 4–8 weeks and involves submitting original academic documents. Flygrad guides you through the entire APS process.

Block Account: Everything You Need to Know

€11,208 (₹10.7L) / year

A blocked account (Sperrkonto) proves you can support yourself financially in Germany. You deposit €11,208 (₹10.7L) (~₹10 lakh) in a German bank before applying for the visa. After arrival, €934 is released each month.

Providers: Fintiba, Expatrio, Deutsche Bank — account setup typically takes 1–2 weeks online.

18-Month Job Seeker Visa

After graduating, you can apply for an 18-month job seeker visa (Aufenthaltserlaubnis zur Jobsuche). This allows you to stay in Germany and search for work matching your qualification. Germany has 2+ million job vacancies — especially in engineering, IT, and healthcare.

Once employed with a salary above €45,300/year (STEM: €41,041), you qualify for the EU Blue Card — the fastest PR pathway in Germany, requiring only 21 months (with B1 German) or 33 months to permanent residence.

Study in Canada from India — PR Pathway, SDS Visa & University Guide 2025

3-year PGWP, Express Entry PR in 2–3 years, SDS fast-track visa in 20 days — the definitive guide for Indian students choosing Canada.

Why Canada is the #1 PR destination

Canada's Post-Graduate Work Permit (PGWP) and Express Entry system create the world's most direct pathway from student to permanent resident. Most Indian students achieve PR within 4–5 years of starting their degree.

📌 SDS — Student Direct Stream (20-day visa)

Indian students can apply under the Student Direct Stream (SDS) for a much faster processing time of approximately 20 days. Requirements: IELTS 6.0+, Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) of CAD 10,000, and a letter of acceptance from a DLI.

The 3-Year PGWP Explained

After graduating from a 2-year program at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI), you receive a 3-year open work permit. This means you can work anywhere, for any employer in Canada — no sponsorship needed.

After 1 year of skilled work experience, you qualify for Canadian Experience Class (CEC) under Express Entry — one of the most points-rich PR pathways available to Indian nationals.

UK Student Visa Guide for Indians 2025 — CAS, Funds & Graduate Route

CAS letter, 28-day rule for bank statements, Graduate Route 2-year visa, UCAS vs direct applications — the complete guide for Indian students.

The UK Student Visa — What Indian Students Must Know

The UK Student Visa (formerly Tier 4) requires a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your university, financial proof, and an IELTS score. Unlike Germany, there is no APS certificate requirement for Indian students.

📌 The 28-Day Bank Rule

Your bank account must show the required funds (tuition + £1,334/month for London; £1,023/month outside London) consistently for 28 consecutive days before your visa application date. This is one of the most common visa rejection reasons.

Graduate Route — 2-Year Work Visa

After graduating from any UK university, you can apply for the Graduate Route visa — 2 years of open work rights for Bachelor's and Master's graduates, and 3 years for PhD graduates. No job offer required, and you can work in any occupation at any salary level.
