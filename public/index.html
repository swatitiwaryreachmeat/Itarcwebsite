FAQs

Questions students ask most

Is ITARC's counselling completely free?+
Yes, 100% free. Zero cost, zero obligation. We believe you deserve honest expert guidance before any financial commitment. Fees only apply if you choose full application support.
Can I study in Germany for free?+
Yes. German public universities charge no tuition fees for international students — only a semester contribution of €150–350 (roughly ₹14,000–30,000). Living costs are the main expense.
What IELTS score do I need?+
Ireland and UK: 6.0–6.5. Netherlands and Sweden: 6.0+. Germany English programs: 6.5. Germany German-language programs need TestDaF. We'll confirm the exact score needed for your target universities.
What post-study work options exist?+
Ireland: 2-year stay-back permit. UK: 2-year Graduate Route visa. Germany: 18-month job seeker visa. France & Netherlands: 1-year orientation permits. Sweden also allows job searching after graduation.
How long does the full process take?+
Typically 4–9 months from first counselling to visa approval. September intake: start November–January. January intake: start July–August. We accommodate late starters — contact us to map your timeline.
Does ITARC help with education loans?+
Yes. Click the "Education Loan" tab above for full details. We guide you through the complete loan process — documentation, bank selection, and disbursement timelines.

Loan types

Which loan is right for you?

🏦
Secured Education Loan
Backed by collateral (property, FD, insurance). Lower interest rates. Best for large loan amounts above ₹40L.
  • Interest rate: 8.5–10.5% p.a.
  • Loan amount: up to ₹1.5 Cr
  • Collateral: property, FD, LIC
  • Tax benefit under Section 80E
  • Best for: Germany, UK, Ireland
📋
Unsecured Education Loan
No collateral required. Faster processing. Available from NBFCs and select banks. Best for students without property assets.
  • Interest rate: 10–12.5% p.a.
  • Loan amount: up to ₹50L
  • No collateral required
